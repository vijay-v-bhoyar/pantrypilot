# Design and information architecture

Warm cream #F7F7F2 background, white surfaces, dark ink #183F3B, teal #176C61 primary actions; restrained coral #B95136 highlights. Color never carries status alone. Body >=16px; visible focus; sufficiently large touch targets. WCAG AA contrast and keyboard flows.

## Screens

Public home/campaign directory; nonprofit campaign detail; donation/recurring checkout; donor history/receipt/subscription management. Staff overview with readiness/funds/activity; campaigns editor/report; inventory/lots/import/transfer/kit definitions; procurement request/proposal/comparison/approval/checkout; supplier catalog/orders/fulfillment; recovery case/timeline/refund/replacement; Studio workspace/custom widgets/saved dashboards; members/sites/settings/connection status; audit/reconciliation/health; isolated guided judge welcome.

Dense operational tables retain readable typography, labels and filters. Donor pages emphasize purpose, confirmed campaign progress and payment trust. Responsive donor/approval flows at 390px; full staff layouts at desktop, mobile cards/scroll areas without page overflow. Every action acknowledges completion, persistence or uncertainty.

Approve dialogs show exact items/cost/merchant/fund/restrictions/change consequences. Error states retain form input. Empty states offer real next actions. Loading states avoid invented metrics. Sandbox and simulation/real-provider labels stay visible. No misleading tax, distributed-kit or AI-success claims. Explanations cite inputs and concise decision reasons, not private model reasoning.

Role workflow guidance is an optional native details disclosure, with 16px text, keyboard activation and 44px summary/button targets. It resets on role change and offers only existing role-permitted navigation. It never marks steps complete or executes a financial action. Donor history distinguishes failed refund metadata from financially pending state, explains no confirmed refund and directs review of the existing resource; no second gift is a refund retry. Human comprehension and preference validation remain NOT_RUN.
